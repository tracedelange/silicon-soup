How overlays work
  1. Paint in the five key grays only. Each maps to the equipped material's ramp stop at the same index — step 0 is the outline, step 4 is specular highlight.
  2. No color: the material supplies it, a brand tints the top two stops, and rarity rings the silhouette.
  3. Full width, 64×64 — a weapon is asymmetric, so it can't use the body's left-half mirror.
  4. Align to the anchors marked on the grid — the magenta bands are the body's landmark rows, and the cyan boxes are the hand columns a grip has to land in.
Saving writes client/public/gear/<layer>.png. There's no registry to update — the file appearing is what makes an overlay real. Prefer Aseprite? Grab the pose guide and the palette at gear/keys.gpl.
